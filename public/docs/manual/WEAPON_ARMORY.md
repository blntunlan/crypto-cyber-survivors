# :Swords: Weapon Arsenal

> **Status** live
> Clearance: Level 1 (Public)

## The Armory

Your combat chassis can equip up to 6 different weapons simultaneously. Each weapon can be upgraded up to Level 5 by collecting experience gems. 

Every weapon in your arsenal reacts differently to the live crypto market data.

### Quantum Bullet
- **Type**: Rapid-fire kinetic projectile.
- **Description**: Your default starting weapon. Fires in the direction you are facing.
- **Market Interaction**: Standard performance. Reliable in all market conditions.

### Spread Shot
- **Type**: Multi-directional fan attack.
- **Description**: Fires multiple projectiles in a wide arc. Excellent for crowd control.
- **Market Interaction**: **Volume Bonus**. When market trading volume spikes, this weapon fires additional projectiles.

### Volatility Laser
- **Type**: Continuous piercing beam.
- **Description**: Slices through multiple enemies in a straight line.
- **Market Interaction**: **ATR Scaling**. The damage of this laser scales linearly with market volatility. When the market goes crazy, this laser melts everything.

### RSI Boomerang
- **Type**: Returning physical projectile.
- **Description**: Thrown outwards, then returns to the player, hitting enemies twice.
- **Market Interaction**: **Trend Bonus**. If your current PnL is positive (you are winning your trade), the boomerang doubles in size and damage.

### AOE Nuke
- **Type**: Explosive ordinance.
- **Description**: Fires a slow-moving bomb that explodes in a massive radius.
- **Market Interaction**: **Bailout Protocol**. If your PnL drops below -30% (you are losing badly), the nuke deals 1.5x damage to help clear the screen and keep you alive.

## Weapon Evolutions

When you manage to upgrade two specific, compatible weapons to their maximum level (Level 5), they will fuse together during your next level-up to create a devastating **Ultimate Weapon**.

**Known Combinations:**
- `Quantum Bullet (Lv.5) + Volatility Laser (Lv.5) = Hyper Cannon`
- *(More combinations are being discovered by operators daily...)*
