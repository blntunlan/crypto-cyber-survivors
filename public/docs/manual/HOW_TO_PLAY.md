# :Gamepad2: Survivor Protocol

> **Status** live
> Clearance: Level 1 (Public)

## Mission Directive

Welcome to the Arena. Your objective is simple: **Survive**. 
The environment is not static; it is physically linked to the live fluctuations of the Bitcoin (BTC/USD) market. As the market breathes, the Arena transforms.

## Core Controls

You are operating an advanced combat chassis. Movement is your primary defense. Firing is handled automatically by the onboard targeting computer.

**Desktop (Keyboard / Mouse)**
- `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Arrow Keys`: Move your character.
- `Spacebar` or `Shift`: Execute a high-speed Dash (Brief invulnerability window).
- `Escape`: Pause the simulation and open the terminal menu.

**Mobile (Touch Interface)**
- **Left Side**: Drag to move using the Virtual Joystick.
- **Right Side**: Tap the Dash button to evade incoming threats.

## The Loop: Survive, Collect, Evolve

1. **Evade & Destroy**: Enemies will continuously spawn and swarm your position. Keep moving to avoid taking damage.
2. **Harvest Crypto-Gems**: Fallen enemies drop experience gems. Collect these to power up your chassis.
3. **Level Up**: When your experience bar fills, time will slow down. You will be presented with 3 random upgrades. Choose wisely to build your arsenal.
4. **Market Events**: Keep an eye on the HUD. When market volatility (ATR) spikes, or when the trend shifts (RSI), the game rules will change instantly.

## The Hub

Between runs, you will return to the Hub. Here you can:
- **Spend Gold**: Use the gold you collected during your runs to purchase permanent *Meta-Upgrades* (More HP, faster movement, higher critical hit chance).
- **Check Leaderboards**: See how your survival times stack up against other operators worldwide.
- **Review Telemetry**: Analyze your past performance and trading PnL (Profit and Loss) during your runs.
