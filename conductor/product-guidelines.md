# Product Guidelines: Crypto Survivors

## 1. Tone and Voice
- **Technical & Intense:** The project's written communication should mirror the high-adrenaline, high-stakes nature of the crypto markets. Use finance-heavy terminology (e.g., "Liquidation," "Volatility," "PnL," "Leverage") to reinforce the theme.
- **Expert & "Diamond-Hands":** Position the game as a challenge for those who understand market dynamics. Use trader slang and celebrate high-risk, high-reward strategies to build a niche, loyal community.
- **Direct & Sharp:** Avoid fluff. UI text and marketing should be concise and impactful, reflecting the fast pace of survival gameplay.

## 2. Visual Identity
- **High-Contrast Terminal Aesthetic:** Utilize deep dark backgrounds (blacks and midnight blues) paired with vibrant neon accents (Cyan, Magenta, Gold).
- **Critical Data Emphasis:** Real-time BTC prices, PnL percentages, and health bars must use high-impact neon styling to remain readable during intense combat.
- **Cyber-Finance HUD:** The interface should feel like a high-tech trading terminal, with grid lines and stylized financial indicator visualizations (RSI/ATR) integrated into the game world.

## 3. Gameplay Design Principles
- **Clarity Under Chaos:** Despite the presence of hundreds of enemies and projectiles, the player's position, health status, and primary market metrics must remain distinct and easily readable at all times.
- **Satisfying Micro-Loops:** Every interaction (killing an enemy, collecting XP gems, leveling up) must trigger immediate and satisfying visual/auditory feedback to maintain player engagement and "juice."
- **Rhythmic Flow:** Transitions between gameplay, the "slot-machine" level-up screen, and menus should be fluid and seamless, maintaining the player's flow state without jarring breaks.

## 4. UI/UX Standards
- **Performance First:** All visual effects (screen shake, particles, flashes) must be optimized to ensure a stable 60 FPS across desktop and mobile browsers.
- **Contextual Intelligence:** Use the "Cyber-Finance" theme to explain game mechanics (e.g., a "Liquidation" event instead of a simple "Game Over").
- **Mobile Responsiveness:** Ensure all UI elements are touch-friendly and respect device safe areas (notches/home indicators) without sacrificing the terminal aesthetic.
