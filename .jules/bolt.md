## 2025-10-24 - Zero-Allocation Iteration Optimization
**Learning:** High-frequency physics systems like `MovementSystem.ts` that iterate over object pools thousands of times per second cause significant garbage collection pressure due to `Array.prototype.forEach` closure allocations. While V8 is generally fast, eliminating these micro-allocations in hot loops stabilizes the 60 FPS target by preventing GC pauses.
**Action:** Always replace `Array.prototype.forEach` with standard `for (let i = 0, len = arr.length; i < len; i++)` loops in engine update ticks (like `MovementSystem.ts` or `EntityRenderer.ts`). When iterating over sparse arrays like object pools, strictly include a guard clause (`if (entity === undefined) continue;`) to prevent `TypeError`s, and convert any inner callback `return` statements to `continue`.
