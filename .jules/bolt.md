## 2024-05-15 - Array.forEach closures in hot physics loop
**Learning:** The combat physics loop (`MovementSystem.ts`) uses `Array.prototype.forEach` extensively for iterating over large collections of entities (enemies, particles, bullets, etc.) every frame (60fps). This creates many temporary closure allocations per frame, adding significant garbage collection pressure on mobile devices. Standard `for` loops eliminate this overhead and often execute faster.
**Action:** Replace `Array.prototype.forEach` with standard `for (let i = 0, len = arr.length; i < len; i++)` loops in high-frequency update functions in `MovementSystem.ts`. Make sure to add `if (item === undefined) continue;` to guard against sparse pools if applicable (though `activeXXX` pools usually contain objects, it's safer).
