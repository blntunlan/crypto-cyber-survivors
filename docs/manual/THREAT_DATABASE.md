## Enemy Classifications

The Arena is populated by digital manifestations of market forces. You will encounter several tiers of threats during a run.

### 1. Swarmers (The Retail Traders)
- **Appearance**: Small, numerous, fragile.
- **Behavior**: They spawn in massive clusters, blindly chasing you.
- **Threat Level**: Low individually, High in groups.
- **Market Link**: They become extremely aggressive and fast when the market is Crashing (RSI < 30).

### 2. Elites (The Algorithms)
- **Appearance**: Larger, distinct colors, glowing auras.
- **Behavior**: These enemies move predictably but have significantly higher health and damage. They drop high-value chests when defeated.
- **Threat Level**: Medium.
- **Market Link**: Elite spawn rates increase during high Volatility (ATR spikes).

### 3. Bosses (The Whales)
- **Appearance**: Massive entities. Sometimes surrounded by smaller minions.
- **Behavior**: Bosses spawn at specific time intervals or during extreme market anomalies. They have unique attack patterns (e.g., charging, firing projectiles).
- **Threat Level**: Extreme.
- **Market Link**: Defeating a Whale grants a massive payout of Gold and Meta-Experience.

## Defense Strategies

- **Kiting**: Never walk in a straight line. Move in large circles to gather Swarmers into a tight group, maximizing the damage of your AOE weapons.
- **Corner Traps**: The Arena has boundaries. If you get pinned against a wall by an Elite, use your Dash to phase through them.
- **Target Prioritization**: Focus your fire on Elites to force them to drop a Chest, which can heal you or provide a magnet to collect all gems on the screen.
